# ASD-STE100 writing rules

This file gives the writing rules in a short form. It does not replace the
official ASD-STE100 specification. Get the specification at no cost from
https://asd-ste100.org.

The rule numbers in this file are local IDs for quick reference.

## 1. Words

**1.1** Use only the approved words from the Dictionary. Technical names and
technical verbs are exceptions.

**1.2** Use a word with only its approved part of speech. Example: "test" is a
noun. Use "do a test", not "test the pump".

**1.3** Use a word with only its approved meaning. Example: "follow" means "to
come after", not "to obey".

**1.4** Do not use the same word as a noun and as a verb.

**1.5** Use one term for one thing in all of the text. Do not use synonyms.

**1.6** Do not use a gerund (an "-ing" word) as a noun, as an adjective, or as
a part of a complex verb. A technical name is an exception.
- Not permitted: "Before starting the engine, do a check of the oil."
- Permitted: "Before you start the engine, do a check of the oil."

**1.7** Use the approved forms of the verbs: the infinitive, the imperative,
the simple present tense, the simple past tense, the simple future tense, and
the past participle as an adjective.

**1.8** Do not use a complex verb tense. Example: do not use "has been
removed". Use "was removed" or "is removed".

**1.9** Use a maximum of three nouns in a noun cluster. If the cluster is
longer, use prepositions.
- Not permitted: "runway light approach system control panel"
- Permitted: "the control panel of the approach system for the runway lights"

**1.10** Do not remove articles ("a", "an", "the") to make the text short.

**1.11** Do not use an abbreviation that is not in the official list, unless
you give the full term at the first use.

**1.12** Do not use a slash ("/"). The mark "and/or" is an exception.

**1.13** Write numbers as numerals, not as words, for quantities and
measurements.

## 2. Sentences

**2.1** A procedural sentence has a maximum of 20 words.

**2.2** A descriptive sentence has a maximum of 25 words.

**2.3** Write one instruction in one sentence. If two actions occur at the same
time, you can write them in one sentence with "and".

**2.4** Use the imperative for an instruction. Start the sentence with the
verb.

**2.5** Use the active voice in procedural text. Use the active voice in
descriptive text where possible.

**2.6** Use the passive voice in descriptive text only when the active voice
makes the sentence unclear, or when the agent is not known.

**2.7** Keep the words that go together near to each other.

**2.8** Do not use more than one topic in one sentence.

**2.9** Use a connective word ("and", "but", "or", "because") to show the
relation between two ideas. Do not use a long chain of connectives.

## 3. Procedures

**3.1** Write each step as a separate numbered step.

**3.2** Write a maximum of one instruction in each step. A step can have more
than one sentence if the actions are related.

**3.3** Give the condition before the action.
- Permitted: "If the lamp comes on, stop the pump."

**3.4** Put the warning or the caution before the step that it applies to.

**3.5** Do not put a warning or a caution in the middle of a step.

## 4. Descriptive text

**4.1** Write a maximum of six sentences in a paragraph.

**4.2** Write one topic in one paragraph. Start the paragraph with the topic
sentence.

**4.3** Use a table or a list when you give more than three related items.

## 5. Warnings and cautions

**5.1** Write a warning for a risk of injury or death to persons.

**5.2** Write a caution for a risk of damage to equipment.

**5.3** Start a warning or a caution with a command, or with a clear statement
of the condition.

**5.4** Give the result of the risk, if the result is not obvious.

**5.5** Write warnings and cautions in the imperative. Keep them short.

## 6. Punctuation

**6.1** Use the hyphen only in words that have a hyphen in the Dictionary.

**6.2** Do not use parentheses to add a second idea to a sentence. Write a new
sentence.

**6.3** Use a colon to introduce a list.

## 7. What not to change

**7.1** Do not change a technical name, a part number, a measurement, a
tolerance, or a value to obey a rule.

**7.2** Do not remove safety data to make the text short.

**7.3** If a rule and the technical accuracy have a conflict, keep the accuracy
and tell the user.
